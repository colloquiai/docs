title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from a new account to a live phone call with your first Colloqui agent."
---

By the end of this page you will have an agent answering a real phone number. It takes about fifteen minutes and needs nothing but a browser and a phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [dashboard.colloqui.ai](https://dashboard.colloqui.ai/register) with your name, email and a password, then confirm the verification email. Your account comes with a workspace, which is where agents, numbers and billing live. You can invite teammates to it later from **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a payment method">
    Open **Billing** in the sidebar and add a card. Colloqui bills per workspace, and the dashboard will not let you edit an agent or add a number until a payment method is on file.

    <Note>
    If you skip this step you will see a **Payment method required** banner on the Agents page. The **Configure Payment Method** button on that banner takes you to the same place.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Go to **Agents**, click **Create Agent**, and choose how it should be built:

    - **Single Prompt** drives the whole call from one prompt. Start here.
    - **Conversation Flow** is a visual flow of nodes with explicit transitions. Use it once you need a call to follow a fixed structure, such as collecting details in order before a booking.

    The agent opens as a draft called **New Agent**. It already has a working language model, voice and transcription configured, so you can make a call before you change anything.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the prompt">
    On the **Prompt** tab, replace the placeholder in **Global Prompt** with what the agent should do. A short, concrete prompt beats a long vague one. For a first test:

    ```text
    You are the receptionist for Harbour Dental. Greet the caller, find out
    whether they want to book, change or cancel an appointment, and collect
    their full name and phone number. Ask one question at a time. When you
    have what you need, tell the caller the team will call them back to
    confirm, then end the call.
    ```

    In the same tab you can set the **Welcome Message**, which is the first thing the caller hears. Leave it on the default for now.

    Click **Save**. Saving updates the draft only; nothing is live yet.

    <Tip>
    See [Prompt engineering](/core-concepts/prompt-engineering) for the patterns that make voice prompts reliable, and [Prompt examples](/core-concepts/prompt-examples) for complete prompts you can adapt.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a phone number">
    Open **Phone Numbers** and click **Add Number**. You have two options:

    - **Buy Number** shows numbers available to purchase. Pick one and give it a nickname such as `Harbour Dental main line`.
    - **Add Number** registers a number you already own. You will need to route that number to Colloqui at your carrier. See [Numbers](/deploy/numbers) for the details.

    Buying a number is the quickest path for a first test.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign the number to your agent">
    Select the number you just added. Under **Inbound Agent**, choose your agent and save.

    Assigning a number publishes the agent automatically. You will see a new version in the agent's version history described as *Assigned to* followed by the number's nickname. An agent cannot be published without at least one number, so this is the step that makes it live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the call">
    Dial the number from your phone. You should hear the welcome message, and the agent will follow your prompt from there.

    Once you hang up, open **Call History**. The call appears with its transcript, and a recording if recording is enabled, which it is by default on new agents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing the agent after it is live

Edits to a published agent are saved to its draft and do not affect calls until you publish them. When you are ready, click **Publish**, add a short description of what changed, and confirm. The agent's version history keeps every published version, and you can restore an earlier one at any time.

## What a new agent starts with

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Language model | GPT-4.1 |
| Transcription | Deepgram Nova 3 |
| Voice | ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 |
| First speaker | The agent, with a generated welcome message |
| Call recording | On |
| Maximum call length | 60 minutes |
| Ring time before answering | 30 seconds |

All of these can be changed on the agent page. See [Build an agent](/build-an-agent/llm) for what each setting does.

title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from a new account to a live phone call with your first Colloqui agent."
---

By the end of this page you will have an agent answering a real phone number. It takes about fifteen minutes and needs nothing but a browser and a phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [dashboard.colloqui.ai](https://dashboard.colloqui.ai/register) with your **Name**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**, then confirm the verification email. Your account comes with a workspace named after you, which is where agents, numbers, team members and billing live. You can rename it and invite teammates later under **Settings → Workspace**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a payment method">
    Open **Billing** in the sidebar and add a card. Colloqui bills per workspace, and you cannot create or save an agent until a payment method is on file.

    <Note>
    Until you do, the Agents page shows a **Payment method required** banner, and an open agent shows "Changes to this agent cannot be saved until one is configured." The **Configure Payment Method** button on either takes you to the billing portal.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Go to **Agents**, click **Create Agent**, and choose how it should be built:

    - **Single Prompt**: one prompt drives the whole conversation. Start here.
    - **Conversation Flow**: the conversation is a graph of nodes on a canvas. Use it once a call must follow a fixed structure, such as collecting details in order before a booking.

    Click **Create Agent**. The agent opens as a draft called **New Agent**, with a working language model, voice and transcription already configured. Click the pencil next to the name to **Rename Agent**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the prompt">
    Replace the placeholder in **Global Prompt** with what the agent should do. A short, concrete prompt beats a long vague one. For a first test:

    ```text
    You are the receptionist for Harbour Dental. Greet the caller, find out
    whether they want to book, change or cancel an appointment, and collect
    their full name and phone number. Ask one question at a time. When you
    have what you need, tell the caller the team will call them back to
    confirm, then end the call.
    ```

    Under **Welcome Message** you choose who speaks first (**AI speaks first** or **User speaks first**) and whether the greeting is a **Static message** you type or a **Dynamic message based on prompt**. Leave the defaults for now: the agent speaks first with a generated greeting.

    Click **Save**. Saving updates the draft only; nothing is live yet.

    <Tip>
    See [Prompt engineering](/core-concepts/prompt-engineering) for the patterns that make voice prompts reliable, and [Prompt examples](/core-concepts/prompt-examples) for complete prompts you can adapt.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a phone number">
    Open **Phone Numbers** and click **Add Number**. The menu has two options:

    - **Buy Number** lists Australian numbers available to purchase. Pick one under **Number** and give it a **Nickname** such as `Harbour Dental main line`.
    - **Add Number** registers a number you already own. You will also need to route that number to Colloqui at your carrier. See [Numbers](/deploy/numbers).

    Buying is the quickest path for a first test. The number is ready as soon as the dialog closes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign the number to your agent">
    Select the number, choose your agent under **Inbound Agent**, and click **Save**.

    Assigning a number publishes the agent automatically. Open **Versions** on the agent page and you will see version 1 described as *Assigned to* followed by the number's nickname. An agent cannot be published without a number, so this is the step that makes it live.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the call">
    Dial the number from your phone. It rings for a moment, then you hear the welcome message and the agent follows your prompt from there. Ask to book an appointment, give a name and number, and let it end the call.

    Once you hang up, open **Call History**. The call appears with its agent, time, duration and cost. Open it to read the **Transcription**, play the recording (on by default for new agents) and see any extracted **Data**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Changing the agent after it is live

Edits to a published agent are saved to its draft and do not affect calls until you publish them. When you are ready, click **Publish**, add a short **Description** of what changed, and confirm. **Versions** keeps every published version, and you can **Restore** an earlier one into the draft at any time. See [Versioning and publishing](/versioning/overview).

## What a new agent starts with

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Language model | GPT-4.1 (Azure), temperature 0.3 |
| Transcription | Deepgram Nova 3 |
| Voice | ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 |
| First speaker | The agent, with a generated welcome message |
| Tools | One End Call tool that says "Thank you for calling. Goodbye!" |
| Semantic guardrails | On |
| Call recording | On |
| Maximum call length | 60 minutes |
| Ring time before answering | 30 seconds |

All of these can be changed on the agent page. See [Agents](/core-concepts/agents) for every setting.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I test the agent from my browser with a microphone?">
    No. There is no browser test call. Test by dialling the agent's number from a phone, which is also the only way to hear real latency, interruptions and line quality. Each test call appears in **Call History** with a transcript and recording, so you can review it afterwards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a phone number?">
    Yes. An agent cannot be published, and therefore cannot take a call, until at least one number is assigned to it. Assigning the number is what publishes it. If you later remove the agent's last number, it goes back to draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a bundle before the first call?">
    The agent page has a **Choose a Bundle** button. A bundle is the monthly plan an agent runs on, with an included allowance and a per-minute rate beyond it. See [Billing](/workspace/billing) for how bundles and number charges work together.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Buying a number failed with a payment error">
    **Payment failed. Please try again.** means the card on file was declined when the number was added to your subscription. Open **Billing**, check the card in the billing portal, then try again. Nothing is charged for a number that was not created.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which countries and regions are numbers available in?">
    Colloqui numbers are Australian. **Buy Number** offers numbers from the available pool, and **Add Number** accepts a valid Australian number. If the pool shows **No numbers available**, contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I called and nothing answered">
    Check three things: the number has an **Inbound Agent** set; the agent shows as published (open **Versions** and confirm there is a current version); and, for a number you brought yourself, your carrier is routing it to Colloqui. See [Numbers](/deploy/numbers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent talks over me or waits too long">
    Both are settings on the agent under **Realtime Transcription Settings**: **Interruption Sensitivity** controls how easily the caller can cut in, and **Response Eagerness** how quickly the agent replies after the caller pauses. See [Speech-to-text](/build-an-agent/stt).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
